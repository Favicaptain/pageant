The Platform

A Cultural & Leadership Platform

Face of Diaspora Global is an international cultural and leadership platform that brings together individuals from diaspora communities across the world. The initiative celebrates identity, cultural heritage and global representation while promoting meaningful engagement between Africa and its global diaspora.

Cultural Exchange

Showcasing our rich cultural heritage, representation and excellence through a prestigious pageant and awards event.

Diaspora Engagement

Bringing together Nigerians in Diaspora and at home to connect, collaborate and highlight contributions to global development.

Cultural Diplomacy

Positioning Nigeria as a central meeting point for global diaspora dialogue, cultural diplomacy and international collaboration.

The Pageant · 2027

Beauty Beyond Borders

An international beauty pageant created to celebrate African beauty in the Diaspora, promote culture and tourism, and empower young women as global ambassadors. The 2027 edition brings together contestants from Nigeria, Ghana, South Africa, the UK, USA, Canada and Dubai.

Objectives
01

Empower Young African Women

Build confidence, leadership and global opportunity for young African women everywhere.

02

Promote Culture & Tourism

Showcase African heritage, destinations and creative industries to a global audience.

03

Create Global Networks

Open doors to international networking, business and career opportunities.

04

Support the Girl Child

Champion our charity project — Education for the Girl Child.

Eligibility
  • Female, 18–28 years old
  • Minimum height 5'6"
  • Of African descent (living anywhere in the world)
  • Single, never married, no children
  • Fluent in English
  • Must hold a valid international passport
  • Of good character, no criminal record
